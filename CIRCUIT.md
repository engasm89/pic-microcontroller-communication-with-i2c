## Connections

- I2C bus: `SDA`, `SCL` with pull-up resistors to VCC, common `GND`.
- Device address e.g., `0x68`.

## Diagram (ASCII)

```
 PIC SDA <----> Device SDA (pull-up)
 PIC SCL <----> Device SCL (pull-up)
 GND --------- Common ground
```
